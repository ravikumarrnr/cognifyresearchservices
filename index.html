About

Cognify Research Services was established in December 2025 with a vision to deliver high-quality, technology-driven research consulting and academic support solutions. The organization is officially registered under Goods and Services Tax (GST) on 24 December 2025, ensuring compliance, credibility, and professional service delivery. Cognify Research Services specializes in providing comprehensive research consulting services, including PhD proposal writing, refined research proposal development, systematic literature review support, and end-to-end assistance in drafting high-quality research papers for reputed journals and conferences. In addition, the organization offers implementation support for Artificial Intelligence (AI), Machine Learning (ML), and Deep Learning (DL) models, enabling researchers and institutions to translate theoretical concepts into practical, impactful solutions. The services also extend to structured training programs focused on the effective use of AI tools in research and innovative Teaching-Learning Methodologies (TLM). These programs are designed to enhance research productivity, academic writing quality, and pedagogical effectiveness through the integration of advanced AI technologies. With a commitment to excellence, innovation, and ethical research practices, Cognify Research Services aims to empower researchers, academicians, and organizations to achieve their research and innovation goals efficiently and effectively.
