About Us

Cognify Research Services was established in December 2025 with a vision to deliver high-quality, technology-driven research consulting and academic support solutions. The organization is officially registered under Goods and Services Tax (GST) on 24 December 2025, ensuring compliance, credibility, and professional service delivery.

The organization specializes in PhD proposal writing, refined research proposal development, systematic literature review support, and drafting research papers for high-impact journals and conferences. In addition, implementation assistance for Artificial Intelligence (AI), Machine Learning (ML), and Deep Learning (DL) models is provided to bridge theoretical concepts with practical applications.

Cognify Research Services also offers structured training programs on AI tools for research and Teaching-Learning Methodologies (TLM), enabling academicians and researchers to enhance productivity, innovation, and academic excellence.
